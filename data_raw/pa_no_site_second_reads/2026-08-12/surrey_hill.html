Surrey Hill Apartments

Uniontown, Pennsylvania

About the Community

This family friendly complex is comprised of 20 two-bedroom and 50 three-bedroom units layered under the Tax Credit and Project Based Section 8 programs. It is located near Uniontown Hospital and local shopping plaza's and restaurants. There are community spaces and laundry amenities onsite with full and part time maintenance staff whom are on call 24/7 for emergency support. Supportive Services Coordinators work alongside management to provide monthly activities for all ages and help to educate the residents on all programs and services available throughout the community.
