# TDHCA HTC Property Inventory snapshot — 2026-08-12

This directory freezes the Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs
(TDHCA) `HTC Property Inventory` workbook retrieved on 2026-08-12 from:

<https://www.tdhca.texas.gov/sites/default/files/multifamily/docs/HTCPropertyInventory_0.xlsx>

TDHCA links this statewide workbook from its [Multifamily Housing Programs
page](https://www.tdhca.texas.gov/programs/multifamily-housing-programs) as
the `HTC Property Inventory`, dated July 31, 2026. The workbook is the
Department's published inventory of properties financed through its Housing
Tax Credit program. It provides TDHCA number, development name, project
address, city, ZIP code, unit counts, and program fields, making it the
official allocating-agency bulk source used only for Texas candidate screening
in this repository.

`manifest.sha256` records the SHA-256 digest of the exact downloaded bytes.
No values in the workbook have been edited. The inventory is not a decision
record for this project: addresses and coordinates in it are not automatically
accepted as LIHTC sites or approved for geocoding. An address may be current,
administrative, or management-oriented rather than a physical site address,
and the inventory does not establish exhaustive physical-site scope. It does
not by itself resolve development identity, historical address scope, or a
missing site.
