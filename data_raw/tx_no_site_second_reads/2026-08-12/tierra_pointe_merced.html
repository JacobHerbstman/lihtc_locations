Tierra Pointe Apartments

Tierra Pointe is an 80-unit community comprised of 1, 2, and 3 bedroom units in Karnes City, TX. Community amenities include a clubhouse with a community room, swimming pool, onsite laundry facility, fitness center, resident computer center with Internet access and playground. All units include range, refrigerator with ice maker, microwave, dishwasher, disposal, central heat and air, window coverings, ceiling fans in living areas and bedrooms, full-size washer and dryer connections and private patio or balcony.  All units are cable and Internet ready.

Contact Tierra Pointe: If you would like more information about this community, contact the leasing office at 830.780.3966 or by email at dcano@nrpgroup.com or visit their website.
