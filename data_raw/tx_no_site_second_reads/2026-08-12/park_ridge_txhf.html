Everything you need, all right here.

Welcome home to Park Ridge Apartments, located in the heart of Llano, Texas. Our affordable apartment homes are surrounded by beautiful parks and the Llano River, giving you plenty of outdoor recreation. Nearby shopping, dining, and entertainment make it easy for you to enjoy the things you love. If you are searching for convenience and comfort, then look no further.

Park Ridge Apartments offers three floor plans that were designed with you in mind. Choose from our one, two, and three-bedroom apartments for rent that come equipped with an all-electric kitchen including breakfast bar, pantry, dishwasher, microwave, and refrigerator. Additional apartment features to enjoy include central air and heating, balcony or patio, carpeted floors, walk-in closets, as well as washer and dryer connections. We are pet friendly!

Our community amenities are sure to please with a laundry facility, play area, and on-site and on-call maintenance. Visit our shimmering swimming pool and relax on those warm summer days. You and your family will appreciate the quality time in the picnic area with a barbecue. Call us today and see what makes Park Ridge Apartments the best-kept secret in Llano, TX.
