\begin{abstract}
This project studies the locations of Low-Income Housing Tax Credit
developments. It asks whether projects disproportionately favor poorer
neighborhoods with fewer homeowners inside the same municipality. The
empirical design and analysis have not yet been fixed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Research Question}

Where within municipalities is subsidized rental housing built, and how does
project siting vary with pre-existing neighborhood income and homeownership?
